THE LITTLE DETAILS

Good to know.

Everything you need before making plans at Cassia.

Does it cost anything to reserve a dining table?

No. A dine-in table request is completely free. Our team confirms requests based on availability.

Can I book without creating an account?

Yes. You can send a dine-in or banquet request as a guest. Keep your tracking link to check the request status.

What can I celebrate in the banquet hall?

Birthdays, anniversaries, family parties and smaller gatherings. Tell our team about your occasion in the enquiry form.

Is decoration included with a banquet booking?

Decorations are optional and add ₹1,000 to the event enquiry. Final event arrangements and pricing are confirmed by our team.

Is my reservation confirmed immediately?

Not yet. Booking requests begin as pending. Reception or management will confirm availability and your reservation.
